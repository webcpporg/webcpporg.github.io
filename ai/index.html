llms.txt and libraries.json, for AI agents

AI agents are first-class readers of webcpp: they use what it publishes to find libraries of high quality. The site writes two files for them at its root.

llms.txt

What webcpp is, then a link and a line for every library and every page of the site, after the llms.txt convention.

llms.txt

libraries.json

The catalogue the site is built from, read from every library's meta: its key, name, description, categories and targets, its repository and its page, and, for a port, the original it follows, with its language, version and licence.

libraries.json

What is coming

  • Integrations with the AIs, which bring them within reach of C++ programs.
  • Documentation written for AI developers, beside the documentation for C++ developers.
